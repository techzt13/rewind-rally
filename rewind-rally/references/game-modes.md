# Rewind Rally game modes

Linked from [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

| Mode | Players guess | Best data source | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `classic` | The moment itself ("which day/photo is this?") | Scene and object labels | Any album, kids |
| `time-machine` | The year, season, or month | Capture date | Albums that span many years |
| `where-in-the-world` | The place or region | Location label | Travel albums |
| `mixed` (default) | Rotates the three modes above | All fields | Most groups |

## Scoring

- Correct after Hint 1: 3 points.
- Correct after Hint 2: 2 points.
- Correct after Hint 3: 1 point.
- `time-machine` bonus: +1 for the exact month.

## Player-group tone

- **kids:** short sentences, playful words, no exact dates required.
- **family:** friendly, mixed difficulty (default).
- **adults:** trickier hints, exact year or month expected.

## Host script template

1. "Welcome to Rewind Rally! I'll read up to three hints for each memory."
2. "Shout your guess any time. The earlier you guess right, the more points you get."
3. "After each round, I'll show the real photo."
